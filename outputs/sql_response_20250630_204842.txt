Here is the BigQuery SQL query that answers the question:

```
SELECT c.customer_id, c.company_name, c.contact_name
FROM `retail.customers` c
JOIN `retail.orders` o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
WHERE o.order_date BETWEEN '1998-01-01' AND '1998-12-31'
```

This query joins the `customers` table with the `orders` table on the `customer_id` column, and then filters the results to only include customers who placed an order in 1998. The output will be a list of customer IDs, company names, and contact names for all customers who placed an order in 1998.
